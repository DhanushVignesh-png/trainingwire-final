Auto-Snap Engine

Rule-based (no AI). Detects offers (Rule 2.4/2.7), rejections (Rule 2.8) and board changes, then auto-publishes snaps as author "Auto" the moment a release lands. Toggle off to inject releases silently for manual snapping only.

Your account

Your username is the author name on every snap you file. Changing either needs your current MPIN — the check happens on the server, so it cannot be clicked past. Snaps you have already filed keep the name they went out under; a published alert is a record of what happened.

Keyword Highlights

Comma-separated keywords highlighted in the reader. Money, %, and dates are always highlighted.
